# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Copyplay is for one owner of a private copyparty server who wants a direct Android phone path from a reachable server to video playback.

## Product Purpose

Copyplay connects to one copyparty host, browses folders as a video-focused file tree, and plays videos with strong local-player controls, subtitle support, and progress resume.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, practical, media-native.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a streaming-service catalog, a movie metadata library, a generic SaaS dashboard, or a decorative onboarding app. Avoid posters, recommendations, ads, and marketing language.

## Design Principles

- Make the active server and playback path obvious.
- Keep browsing file-first and uncluttered.
- Show useful operational state instead of decorative empty space.
- Preserve phone ergonomics and native Android behavior.
- Let playback controls follow mature video-player conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, system text scaling, clear touch targets, reduced visual clutter, and Android-native controls where practical.
